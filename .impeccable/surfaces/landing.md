# Surface Brief: Landing Page

## Scope
Single-page landing for Ezequiel Videla's custom software business. Persuade mode.

## Audience
Dueños y encargados de PyMEs argentinas: comercios, profesionales/servicios, gastronomía. Personas que resuelven ventas, stock, turnos y pedidos a mano y quieren digitalizar sin adoptar software genérico.

## Job
Visitor arrives, understands what Ezequiel does and for whom, sees proof by sector, and reaches out via WhatsApp.

## Action
Click WhatsApp CTA with prefilled sector message.

## Proof
Working mockups per sector (comercios, profesionales, gastronomía), process transparency (3 steps), direct access to Ezequiel.

## Constraints
- WhatsApp only (no forms, no email)
- Spanish rioplatense, pesos argentinos
- GitHub Pages hosting (static)
- No fabricated testimonials or real client data
- Demo data must be labeled

## Direction Contract

**THESIS:** Replace the developer-centric "Kinetic Obsidian" with a warm, handcrafted aesthetic that speaks to business owners as a trusted artisan, not a code technician. The site should feel like walking into a well-run local business — organized, warm, personal — not like looking at a developer's portfolio.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cerámica Artesanal. Chalk-white ground (#FAF7F2) with cobalt blue accents (#2B5EA7), warm terracotta for CTAs (#C17849), sage green for positive states (#7A9E7E). Plus Jakarta Sans display (rounded, friendly), Inter body. NO monospace in main layout. Cards with soft shadows and generous rounded corners. Texture from ceramic tile patterns and handmade quality.

**STORY:** The visitor sees their own business problems (stock, caja, turnos, pedidos) solved in a warm, approachable way. They understand "esto es para mí, no para empresas grandes" and feel comfortable reaching out to a real person, not a corporation.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Fixed clean nav (name, sector links, WhatsApp CTA). Hero: personal headline "Tu negocio merece funcionar mejor", supporting text about the problem, two CTAs (WhatsApp primary + ver soluciones). NO dashboard mockups in hero — save those for sector sections. The hero sells the relationship, not the technology.

**FORM:** Code-led build. Established world replacement. Persuade mode, static Vue port.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
